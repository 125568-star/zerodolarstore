How Zero Dollar Store works

🔎

1. Find something you want

Browse free listings by category and suburb. Check photos, condition and collection details.

💬

2. Message the owner

Sign in and arrange a safe, convenient pickup. No checkout, transfers, postage or payment screens.

🎁

3. Collect it for $0

Agree on a local pickup time and collect your item. Have unwanted stuff? List it for $0 too.

🛡️

Zero payment requests. Zero exceptions.

Listings cannot ask for money, shipping charges, deposits, swaps for cash or handling fees. Report anything suspicious. Free-item giveaways usually use local pickup. Wanted requests can arrange free pickup or free delivery, but no cash, paid shipping or delivery fees.

Be safe when collecting items.

Choose suitable public places where practical, check an item's condition before collection, and never send money. Read all safety rules →
